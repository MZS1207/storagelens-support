Privacy policy

StorageLens for macOS · Updated 16 October 2026

Your files stay on your Mac

StorageLens inspects folders you approve and compares supported duplicate files locally. File contents, paths, sizes and comparison hashes are not sent to the developer. The app has no accounts, advertising, tracking, analytics or network service.

Folder access and local records

The app uses macOS folder permissions. It can remember an inspection folder through a local security-scoped bookmark. Use Forget folder to remove that saved inspection access. Safe Trash requires you to approve your Home folder separately; this approval is kept only for the current app session.

Cleanup audit records are stored in the app’s local sandbox container under Application Support/StorageLens/StoreTrashLogs. Records include selected file paths, completed moves, reported Trash paths and failures. File names may contain personal information. These records are not uploaded. Local settings and audits remain until you remove them; removing the app does not necessarily remove its macOS container or backups.

Trash and recovery

Only individually selected and confirmed files are moved using macOS Trash. StorageLens does not empty Trash. Finder and macOS manage recovery and removal. Review Trash before emptying it yourself.

Purchases and system diagnostics

Apple processes the one-time app purchase. StorageLens does not receive payment card details. Apple may collect App Store or system diagnostics according to your Apple and macOS settings. See Apple’s privacy policy.

Support

If you email support, we use the information you choose to provide to answer your request. Redact private file names and personal data in screenshots or audit records before sharing them. Support emails are separate from the app’s local-only operation.

Changes and contact
