StorageLens support

Inspect storage. Review clutter. Keep control.

Inspect a folder

Open Inspect and choose a folder. Review its largest contents, filter results, open scanned folders or reveal files in Finder. A + marks an incomplete result; unreadable and cloud-only contents can affect totals.

Use Safe Trash

Approve your Home folder, choose Reviewed files, Duplicates or Old logs, then refresh. Select files individually and review the confirmation before proceeding. No files are selected automatically. Close applications editing selected files.

Reviewed files include recognized PDF, TXT, PNG, JPEG, GIF and MP4/MOV/M4V files unchanged for more than seven days in your personal folders. Duplicates protects one exact copy in each verified group. Old logs includes recognized crash reports and dated numbered log rotations over 30 days old, not active logs.

Why is something missing?

Projects, databases, apps, archives, unsupported file formats, developer caches, Docker data, simulators, snapshots, cloud-only files and recently changed files are protected. Only regular local files are eligible. The app is intentionally conservative and may skip files. Review the protected or unavailable locations disclosure.

Restore a file

Open Trash in Finder, select the item and use Put Back when available. StorageLens never empties Trash. Space is not immediately freed by moving files to Trash; review its contents before emptying it yourself.

Access and interrupted operations

Reapprove the folder if permission expires. If a file changes or an operation fails, StorageLens stops subsequent moves and reports completed work. Review the result and Trash before refreshing. Do not assume all selected files moved.

Purchase and compatibility

StorageLens requires macOS 14 or later and supports Apple silicon and Intel Macs. The App Store edition is a one-time purchase with no subscription. Redownload using the Apple Account that bought the app. Billing and refunds are handled by Apple purchase support.
